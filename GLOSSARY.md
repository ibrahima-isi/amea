# AEESGS Membership

Language for the association’s public website and staff administration.

## Language

**Member**:
An AEESGS participant who is a Guinean pupil, student, or intern in Senegal. “Member” describes the audience across all three categories.
_French interface label_: Adhérent
_Avoid_: Customer

**Staff user**:
A person with an administration account, subject to the permissions assigned to that account. A staff user is distinct from a member completing registration.
_Avoid_: Member account

**Public website**:
The association’s visitor-facing space for introduction, community photography, registration, and secure member completion workflows.

**Administration**:
The staff-facing space for managing member records, reviewing documents, communicating, and maintaining association content.
_Also called_: Back-office
