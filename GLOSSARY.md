# AEESGS Membership

Language for the association’s public website and staff administration.

## Language

**Member**:
An AEESGS participant who is a Guinean pupil, student, or intern in Senegal. “Member” describes the audience across all three categories.
_French interface label_: Adhérent
_Avoid_: Customer

**Staff user**:
A person with an administration account, subject to the permissions assigned to that account. A staff user is distinct from a member completing registration.
_Avoid_: Member account

**Public website**:
The association’s visitor-facing space for introduction, community photography, registration, and secure member completion workflows.

**Administration**:
The staff-facing space for managing member records, reviewing documents, communicating, and maintaining association content.
_Also called_: Backoffice

**Association journal**:
The AEESGS public information space covering upcoming activities, reports on past activities, and association announcements.
_French interface label_: Journal de l’amicale

**Upcoming activity**:
An association activity announced before it takes place to inform the community.
_French interface label_: Activité à venir

**Activity report**:
A publication describing an activity that has already taken place.
_French interface label_: Compte rendu d’activité

**Announcement**:
Official information the association wants to share with its community.
_French interface label_: Annonce

**Journal draft**:
A publication prepared for the association journal that is not publicly accessible.
_French interface label_: Brouillon du journal

**Archived activity**:
An ended activity whose page remains available in the journal without appearing among upcoming activities. Archiving does not turn it into an activity report.
_French interface label_: Activité archivée

**Member communication**:
A staff-authored message sent from the **Administration** to a filtered audience of **Members**, distinct from automated transactional notifications.
_French interface label_: Communication
_Avoid_: Blast, newsletter

**Communication recipient**:
An individual **Member** tracked for delivery within a **Member communication**.
_French interface label_: Destinataire

**Consent reminder**:
A **Member communication** inviting **Members** to accept or reconfirm the association’s terms and privacy policy via a personal link on the **Public website**.
_French interface label_: Rappel de consentement CGU

**Deletion request**:
A request submitted by a **Member** after refusing the terms and privacy policy, asking the **Administration** to remove their personal record.
_French interface label_: Demande de suppression
